Why middle-income economies get stuck, and what tips them out

Each point on the map is an economy, described by how many people can run a modern firm (across) and how productive modern firms are (up). From the shaded region, economies drift into a trap with most workers informal; from the rest, they reach a modern state. Change the economy's structure, pick a starting point and a shock, and see where it ends up.
