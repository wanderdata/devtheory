A year counts as covered when all seven sectors are present. Survey years use one source per year (labour force survey preferred); the source codes are ILO's (BA = labour force survey, AA = population census, HA/HB = other household surveys, DA = official estimates).

## Summary: what a decomposition can rest on

*LP years* = years with both value added (7 sectors) and employment (7 sectors). *Survey-anchored* = share of modelled years that are also usable survey years; the rest are ILO interpolation or extrapolation. *Model vs survey* = mean absolute gap between modelled and survey employment shares in common years, in percentage points (max in brackets). *Model/survey total* = median ratio of modelled to survey total employment in common years. *Six-group-only years* = survey years with ILO's six broad groups but no usable 7-sector detail.
